## FAQ

**How does it know what I'm overspending on?**
you define your exact usecase and the site runs background checks on every platform you mentioned + platforms that are available on the market and compare them both to see whether youre overspendding or not

**Do I have to connect my billing accounts?**
not really, you only enter your stack in a short form or choose options from dropdown it returns a report. we don't read your invoices or anything

**Is it actually free, or is this a trial?**
The auditing is completely free, you can make as many audits as you want (note that site has rate limits)

**accurate are the savings numbers?**
Every number is based on current public pricing from the actual vendors. In some cases we estimate savings conservatively, especially when replacing or consolidating overlapping tools, because real teams rarely cut spend perfectly overnight.

**My team is small — is this even useful?**
Honestly, smaller teams usually waste more per seat. most savings come from things like paying for the wrong tier, duplicate AI tools, or monthly billing when annual would make more sense.
